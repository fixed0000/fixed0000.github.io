Recognition method & limitations

Experimental local recognition compares strokes with KanjiVG shapes. This is not a general handwriting model. Cursive writing and merged strokes may not be recognized. Stroke order is not graded. Drawings are not transmitted.

Write one character per box. You can scroll the page outside the writing boxes.

Purpose & Features

Built to practice recalling kanji from readings. Includes small vocabulary sets by level, one handwriting box per character, separate recognition and answer evaluation, review, and local progress storage.

Technology & Limitations

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Canvas, Pointer Events, Web Worker, and localStorage. Recognition uses nearest-neighbor shape matching, not a general trained AI model. Uncertain inputs return “Unable to Recognize”. Offline availability is not guaranteed. If storage is unavailable, records last only for this page session.

Vocabulary & Recognition Sources

Three curated practice words per level. Levels are study guidance, not official JLPT vocabulary lists.
