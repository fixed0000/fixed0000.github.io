      How It Works & Limitations

      Strokes are compared locally with an independent KanjiVG shape collection. This is not a general trained handwriting model: cursive forms, merged strokes, and complex characters can fail. Unrecognized inputs are counted separately from incorrect answers.

      Drawings are neither transmitted nor persisted. Progress stays in this browser. A first launch without a network is not guaranteed. Keyboard input is a separate accessible practice mode.

      Classification & Data Sources

      • Kanji Data / KANJIDIC2 — Readings, meanings, and school grades. JLPT levels use Jonathan Waller’s study classifications, not official lists.
      • Official Kanken level requirements — Levels 10–5 use small subsets of new characters for the corresponding elementary grade. They do not reproduce the complete cumulative exam syllabus. Level 4 and above are preparing. JLPT levels are not converted into Kanken levels.
