The Problem of Finding Equivalents in Translating Japanese Communicatives into Russian (based on the Monogatari Series media franchise)

2025 · Completed · An undergraduate thesis comparing conversational expressions in the Monogatari series with their Russian translations to examine how meaning and nuance are conveyed in context.

Purpose

To examine translation equivalence for Japanese conversational expressions.

Materials & Methodology

Comparison of original expressions with translations. The selected works, expression selection criteria, and final conclusions require verification against the thesis.
