About

I studied Japanese translation and interpreting and now work in hotel operations in Kyoto. Here is an overview of my background, learning, and personal projects.

01

Background

From June 2023 to July 2025, I worked on game localization for Fruitbat Factory. I now work at a hotel in Kyoto, handling reservation management, sales and payment reconciliation, and daily reporting.

02

Apr — Aug 2024

University of Tsukuba

Exchange programme

Studied in Japan as an exchange student.

03

How I Approach Work

Beyond responding to day-to-day issues, I find it rewarding to understand their causes and organize information and workflows in ways that help prevent similar problems from recurring.

In my hotel role, I handle day-to-day needs while taking an interest in information management and checks using operational systems. I enjoy making task lists and organizing the points that need to be checked when information is inconsistent or a system issue occurs.

I want to develop my knowledge of IT and data management and contribute to improvements in the systems and operations that support everyday work.

04

Personal Interests

I enjoy learning new things and trying them for myself. I am interested in computers and recently assembled my own PC.

I also use AI-assisted development to create small programs and personal projects for everyday use.

Outside these projects, I enjoy learning foreign languages and visiting new places. I value the chance to encounter different perspectives and environments and gain new experiences.

05

Future Goals

My first priority is to build specialist knowledge and practical experience so that I develop a thorough understanding of the work I am responsible for.

I also want to understand how my area connects with other departments and how work flows through the company as a whole. My goal is to learn to consider problems across departmental boundaries.

Over the longer term, after gaining further experience over the coming years, I would like to develop toward a management role, such as section manager (課長). I hope to support a team through both specialist knowledge and an understanding of day-to-day work.
