Selected fields: meanings, on/kun readings, grade (KANJIDIC2 origin); jlpt_new (Jonathan Waller study lists, not official JLPT). No WaniKani fields are redistributed.
Upstream KANJIDIC2 by EDRDG: CC BY-SA 4.0, https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project and https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html . Attribution: Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group.
Kanken elementary scope: official https://www.kanken.or.jp/kanken/outline/degree.html . 10 to 5 kyu correspond to cumulative elementary grades 1 to 6. Sets use grade membership as a new-character practice subset, not complete examination syllabuses. Grade field follows the older school allocation; only a small stable subset is used. Higher kyu are marked Preparing; no mapping from JLPT is made.
